PrepLedger — Privacy Policy

PrepLedger is published by Çağrı Koç. Contact: 8824charlie@gmail.com.

Release 0.3.1
This App Store build keeps kitchen records on your device. Online accounts, shared cloud workspaces and paid subscriptions are not enabled in this release.

Your operational records
Business details, equipment, temperature readings, checklist answers, names you enter, corrective actions and optional photos provide your workspace. The SQLite database is encrypted and its key is held in the device Keychain. Photos are separate files in the private app area. The publisher does not receive these records automatically. Your business remains responsible for the records and the people whose information it enters.

Optional statistics
Statistics start off. If enabled, daily counts of selected actions and return visits are stored locally. They contain event names, dates, counts, platform, version and build channel, without names, email addresses, business IDs, persistent analytics user/device IDs, temperatures, descriptions or photos. This build has no remote statistics delivery service. You may export counts yourself. Turning statistics off clears unsent counts; pending counts expire after 30 days when the app runs. No advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking are used.

Permissions and exports
Photo-library access and local notifications are optional. Photos are resized without EXIF metadata. PDF, CSV and JSON exports use the system share sheet; you choose recipients and storage. Shared files are outside the app's control. We do not automatically receive exports.

Storage and deletion
Operational records remain on the device until you remove the app or its data. Completed entries are preserved; use a correction event to correct a measurement. To remove local data, use iOS Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage → PrepLedger → Delete App. Remove exported files and device backups separately. Device backup settings may include app files, but the database key is restricted to the original device; a restored database may not open on another device. Keep suitable exports for business continuity. This release creates no online account to delete.

Support and website information
If you email support, Google Mail processes your address and the message you choose to send. We use that correspondence to answer the request and keep it only as needed to resolve it or meet applicable obligations. Our basis is responding to your request and our legitimate interest in providing support. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary staff/customer information. The support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes connection information such as IP addresses for security. These providers may process information outside the UK; their relevant privacy terms apply to those services.

Your rights
Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability for personal information we hold. Contact your business owner about operational records that remain on its device. Contact 8824charlie@gmail.com about support information we hold. You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/. Cloud services will need updated privacy information before they are offered.
