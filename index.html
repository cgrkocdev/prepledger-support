Export your records
Use Reports for PDF and CSV, and Settings → Export workspace data for JSON. Save copies somewhere suitable for your business. Exports include the records available on that device, rather than a separate cloud backup.

Delete local data
Release 0.3.1 does not create an online account. To remove local app data on iPhone or iPad, open iOS Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage → PrepLedger → Delete App. For Android, use the system app storage controls. Remove shared exports and device backups separately. Deleted local records cannot be recovered by the publisher.

Personal information requests
Email support about information sent to us. For operational records held only on your business's device, contact the business owner responsible for them. No fixed legal retention period is claimed for food safety records.

PrepLedger is not affiliated with or approved by the Food Standards Agency.
