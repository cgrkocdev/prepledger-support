Your kitchen records
Use PrepLedger to record your own business checks and actions. You are responsible for accurate entries, suitable measuring equipment, chosen temperature limits, food safety procedures and review of suggested checklists. The app does not measure temperatures itself.

Food safety responsibilities
PrepLedger supports records for food safety and HACCP-based procedures. It is not approved by or affiliated with the Food Standards Agency, does not supply a complete HACCP plan and does not guarantee compliance or an inspection outcome. Suggested checks must be reviewed for your business.

Release 0.3.1 and exports
This release saves records locally without an online account. Paid subscriptions and shared cloud accounts are not active. There is no charge, trial or automatic renewal in the app. Exports reflect the records available on your device at generation time. PDF and CSV include available measurements, corrections, daily summaries and issues known to be open at the selected period end; JSON exports workspace records. Keep suitable exports and your own continuity arrangements. Device loss or removing app data may make local records unavailable; automatic cross-device recovery is not provided.

Record integrity
Committed measurements and completed checks keep their history. Corrections and corrective actions are separate events. Do not record unlawful material, use another business's information without authority, or include unnecessary personal information.
